(specs/rhema-framework-v1.md §7) — do not scale the Deployment.

   kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "rhema-claw.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
   curl http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/healthz

   (claw.yaml, mounted at /config/claw.yaml). db and workspace_root are pinned
   under /data; edit them through values, not the ConfigMap.
